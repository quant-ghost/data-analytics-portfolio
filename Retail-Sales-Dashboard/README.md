
# Retail Sales Dashboard

## Project Overview

An interactive Retail Sales Dashboard developed using Microsoft Excel and Power BI to analyze retail revenue, sales volume, shopping mall performance, and product category distribution.

The dashboard provides a comprehensive overview of retail business performance, transforming raw transaction data into meaningful business insights through interactive visualizations and key performance indicators (KPIs).

## Business Objective

The main objective of this project is to monitor retail sales performance, evaluate revenue trends, compare shopping mall performance, and understand product category contributions.

The dashboard helps identify business trends, evaluate sales performance, and support informed, data-driven decision-making.

## Data Sources & Workflow

The project follows a structured data preparation and visualization workflow:

- **Microsoft Excel:** Used as the primary data source for organizing, cleaning, and preparing retail transaction data.
- **Microsoft Power BI:** Used to import and analyze the prepared dataset, develop visualizations, and build an interactive dashboard.

## Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Excel** – Data cleaning, organization, and preparation.
- **Microsoft Power BI** – Data analysis, visualization, and dashboard development.

## Data Analysis & Preparation

- Organized and structured raw retail transaction data in Excel.
- Cleaned and prepared datasets for Power BI integration.
- Analyzed total revenue and sales quantities.
- Evaluated revenue performance across different shopping malls.
- Compared sales contributions across product categories.
- Analyzed revenue trends across different time periods.
- Developed interactive visualizations and filtering options.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

| Metric | Value | Description |
|---|---:|---|
| Total Income | $101.17M | Total revenue generated across all shopping malls |

## Key Analysis Areas

**1. Revenue by Shopping Mall**

Compares revenue generated by individual shopping malls, helping identify top-performing retail locations.

**2. Revenue by Product Category**

Analyzes revenue distribution across product categories, including Clothing, Shoes, Technology, and other retail segments.

**3. Revenue Trend Analysis**

Tracks revenue performance over the selected date range, helping identify changes, patterns, and fluctuations over time.

**4. Sales Volume Analysis**

Evaluates the total quantity of products sold to provide an overview of retail sales activity.

## Dashboard Features

- KPI cards displaying total income and sales quantity.
- Comparative bar charts for shopping mall revenue.
- Product category revenue distribution using pie/donut charts.
- Revenue trend visualization over time.
- Interactive date range slicer allowing users to select custom reporting periods.
- Shopping mall slicer for location-specific analysis.
- Dynamic filtering and interactive data exploration.

## Business Value

This dashboard provides a centralized view of retail sales performance and helps businesses:

- Identify the highest revenue-generating shopping malls.
- Understand which product categories contribute most to total revenue.
- Monitor revenue trends across selected time periods.
- Evaluate overall sales volume and business performance.
- Identify opportunities for improving retail sales strategies.
- Make informed decisions based on data-driven insights.
